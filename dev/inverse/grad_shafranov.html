Spacecraft Grad-Shafranov Reconstruction ​

reconstruct_grad_shafranov reconstructs a planar magnetohydrostatic strip from a single straight spacecraft crossing. Unlike the spatial-probe methods, it assumes isotropic pressure balance and translational invariance. It is not the cylindrical axisymmetric flux fit.

Measurements and frame ​

  • times contains strictly increasing sample times in seconds.

  • B_observed contains Cartesian magnetic components in tesla.

  • pressure contains nonnegative isotropic thermal pressure in pascals. Missing or non-finite observations are not supported.

  • invariant_axis is the supplied direction of translational invariance.

  • crossing_velocity is the constant spacecraft velocity relative to the structure, in m/s, expressed in the measurement frame.

  • y specifies the increasing transverse strip coordinates in metres, including zero and points on both sides of the track.

The solver does not estimate the de Hoffmann-Teller frame, search for the invariant axis, or determine whether the equilibrium assumptions are valid. Those inputs must be established independently.

Representation and numerical controls ​

In the local frame,

The solver integrates flux along the track, fits P_t(A) and B_z(A) using Chebyshev polynomials, and continues the planar equation in both transverse directions. pressure_degree and axial_degree control the source-function fits; spatial_degree truncates the along-track representation and acts as spatial regularization. max_step limits internal continuation steps.

Evaluation and validation ​

The returned GradShafranovReconstruction evaluates Cartesian positions and returns Cartesian components in the original measurement frame. flux_function, thermal_pressure, and current_density provide derived quantities. Its finite domain is a strip in local x and y; local z is unrestricted.

Elliptic Cauchy continuation is ill-posed. Keep the strip narrow and vary its width, polynomial degrees, and step size. Inspect field.diagnostics for track residuals, source-function extrapolation, and sampled GS residuals. Validate off-track whenever possible: a small track residual does not guarantee stable continuation. Warnings about strip width or flux extrapolation are not quantitative stability or uncertainty bounds.

The interpolated field is divergence-free, but the GS equation is satisfied only approximately. Analytic current is obtained from the interpolated field's curl, not just from the fitted source function.

Example and API ​

The mock spacecraft crossing reconstructs a Fadeev island and compares field, pressure, current, and continuation sensitivity against off-track truth. See reconstruct_grad_shafranov for the complete input contract and Choosing an inverse method for models that do not assume pressure balance.
